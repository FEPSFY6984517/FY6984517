Introduction

  Running shoes hold a historic reputation in its constant development ability and performance. Over successive decades with advancements 
  in technology and material design we have seen running shoes originate from traditional leather sole to 3d printed, carbon plate models. 
  Through the growth in standards of competitive running, the manufacturing market for running shoes has had to closely adapt to stay ahead 
  of the game and competitors. Traditional running attire would be using synthetic textiles on the body of the shoe complete with a foam or 
  spikes set whereas now we are seeing new technologies being implemented such as carbon plating. These rely on three physical principles 
  within material science being; impact force, energy absorption and elasticity.  We will be discussing various popular methods of achieving 
  these factors in running shoes.
